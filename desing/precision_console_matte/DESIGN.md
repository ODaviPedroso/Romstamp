## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes Japanese consumer electronics minimalism with heritage industrial arcade architecture. It departs from nostalgic skeuomorphism and cliché RGB/cyber glow, opting instead for a tactile, physical console feel: deep matte charcoal foundations, high-density neutral surfaces, surgical micro-borders, and razor-sharp typographic hierarchy punctuated by high-energy crimson.

### Target Audience & Persona
Engineered for dedicated gamers, hardware connoisseurs, and modern platform architects who demand low-latency legibility, zero-distraction structural order, and premium industrial tactile feedback across handheld, desktop, and 10-foot interfaces.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Tactile Matte Plasticity:** Surfaces emulate injection-molded, micro-textured polycarbonate shells. Shading relies on subtle tonal steps and fine boundary lines rather than heavy ambient lighting.
- **Engineered Restraint:** Glow effects, noisy glass blurs, and neon gradients are discarded. Energy is communicated through solid, decisive color hits, structural contrast, and instant mechanical state feedback.
- **Physical Precision:** Compact 4px base border radiuses anchor structural panels and hardware cards, while circular pill indicators are reserved exclusively for contextual status chips and console utility triggers.

## Colors

The palette models dark console hardware: absolute clarity over deep, unpolluted matte chassis tones.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#D81B26`):** Precision Crimson. The operational heartbeat of the interface. Used strictly for dominant calls-to-action, primary focus triggers, system notifications, and primary navigational indicators.
- **Secondary (`#FF7A00`):** Arcade Amber. A heritage industrial accent calibrated for secondary badges, reward states, alert sub-tiers, and telemetry metrics.
- **Tertiary (`#2C3035`):** Molded Polycarbonate. Used for secondary structural boundaries, resting tactile surface fills, and structural tab indicators.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#0D0E11`):** Deep Obsidian Matte. Non-reflective chassis backdrop preventing light-bleed and maximizing foreground card contrast.

### Surface System
- **Base Canvas:** `#0D0E11` (Matte frame)
- **Surface Level 1:** `#16181D` (Structural bays, shelves, panels)
- **Surface Level 2:** `#1F2228` (Interactive cards, input modules)
- **Surface Level 3:** `#282C34` (Elevated modals, tooltips, flyout shelves)
- **Stroke Subdued:** `#2E333D` (1px structure separator)
- **Stroke Interactive:** `#434A57` (1px resting interactive edge)
- **Text High-Contrast:** `#F5F6F8` (Headings, active metrics)
- **Text Medium-Contrast:** `#9BA1AD` (Body text, hardware labels, descriptors)
- **Text Subdued:** `#5F6573` (Disabled states, structural metadata)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances technical hardware precision with immediate legibility across multiple viewing distances (handheld to television).

### Type Hierarchy & Pairing
- **Headings (Sora):** A clean, geometric grotesque with wide proportions and architectural corners that match physical hardware bezels. Applied with negative tracking at large scales to preserve density.
- **Body & Controls (Hanken Grotesk):** Highly efficient, non-distracting grotesque optimized for high-density HUD telemetry, inventory matrices, settings lists, and technical descriptions.
- **Caps Labels (`label-caps`):** Rendered in uppercase Sora with expanded tracking (`+0.08em`) to mirror hardware chassis debossing, cartridge markings, and port labels.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an absolute 8px mechanical grid, providing rigid modularity reminiscent of hardware docking stations and operating system game-ribbons.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Compact / Handheld (0–599px):** 4-column fluid layout; 1rem outer margin; 1rem gutter. Content stacks vertically with horizontal card rail snap-scrolling.
- **Medium / Tablet (600–1023px):** 8-column layout; 2rem outer margin; 1.5rem gutter. Split side-rail navigation with 2-to-3 column media matrices.
- **Expanded / Desktop / 10-ft (1024px+):** 12-column layout; 3rem outer margin; 2rem gutter; max canvas width constrained to 1440px to ensure quick spatial scanning.

### Spacing Philosophy
Spacing tokens are structural units, not stylistic padding. Gaps between related hardware cards remain tight (`space-sm` to `space-md`), emphasizing unified mechanical assemblies. Section partitions leverage larger steps (`space-xl` to `space-2xl`) to establish unmistakable architectural zones without resorting to noisy decorative breaks.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects diffused atmospheric drop shadows, neon bloom, and glassy backdrops. Depth is built physically using tonal elevation tiers, crisp 1px mechanical bevels, and tactile inset borders.

### Surface Elevation Steps
- **Layer 0 (Chassis Foundation):** `#0D0E11` baseline canvas.
- **Layer 1 (Recessed Bays & Trays):** `#16181D` accompanied by a 1px solid stroke (`#242831`). Elements look mounted into the main console frame.
- **Layer 2 (Raised Tactile Modules):** `#1F2228` with a 1px boundary (`#343A46`). Micro-depth is achieved through an inset top border highlight: `inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`.
- **Layer 3 (Floating Overlays & System Trays):** `#282C34` bordered with `#434A57`. Supported by a razor-sharp, low-spread dark shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`.

### Tactile Feedback States
Interactive elements utilize physical keypress translation. Resting states feature a subtle bottom border anchor (`border-bottom: 2px solid #00000040`). On active press, the component translates down by 1px with the bottom anchor eliminated, mimicking a physical micro-switch actuation.

## Shapes

The design system uses strict soft-corner industrial architecture (roundedness index `1`).

### Geometry Rules
- **Base Components (`rounded-sm` / 4px):** Applied to cards, dialogue frames, list trays, text inputs, and primary action buttons. Replicates machined plastic injection corners with tight, stable radii.
- **Structural Groups (`rounded-md` / 8px):** Applied to overarching screen partitions, system modals, and full-screen docked panels.
- **Hardware Trigger Exceptions (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Strictly reserved for auxiliary status pills, category indicator tags, game-type chips, and controller gamepad button hints (e.g., [A], [B] utility labels).

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Hardware Button:** Solid `#D81B26` background, `#FFFFFF` text (Sora Title-MD), 4px border radius. Padding: `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Outlined by a crisp 1px highlight edge (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`). Hover state shifts tone to `#EF1E2B`; active state depresses by 1px with background `#B5141E`.
- **Secondary Modular Button:** `#1F2228` fill with 1px border in `#3A414E`. Text in `#F5F6F8`. Focus ring creates a crisp 2px high-visibility Crimson (`#D81B26`) outer offset ring with no blur.
- **Ghost Utility Button:** Transparent fill, `#9BA1AD` text, active border `#D81B26` on focus/hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Hardware Pill Badge:** Pill-shaped (9999px), background `#242831`, border 1px solid `#343A46`, text in `label-caps` (`#F5F6F8`). Padding: `0.25rem 0.75rem`.
- **Arcade Amber Highlight Chip:** Pill-shaped, background `rgba(255, 122, 0, 0.12)`, border 1px solid `#FF7A00`, text `#FF7A00`. Used strictly for event statuses, achievements, and system states.

### Cards & Game Shelves
- Constructed on Surface Level 2 (`#1F2228`) with a continuous 1px outline of `#2E333D` and 4px corner radii.
- Features a top-aligned, 2px concealed status stripe: dormant as transparent, transitioning to solid Precision Crimson (`#D81B26`) upon focus or selection.
- Media elements within cards remain flush to the top edge with zero interior margins and zero corner bleed.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Input trays use recessed Surface Level 1 (`#16181D`), 1px structural stroke (`#2E333D`), and text set to `#F5F6F8`.
- Focus state instantly drops the resting stroke and engages a 1px solid `#D81B26` border accompanied by an inner 1px tactile shadow (`inset 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Checkboxes: 18x18px squares with 3px corners, `#16181D` background, `#3A414E` border. Checked state fills with `#D81B26` displaying a 2px geometric white tick.
- Radios: 18x18px circles, concentric ring layout. Selected state features an exterior border of `#D81B26` and a solid 8px centered Crimson core.

### Lists & Navigation Rails
- Lists function as linked hardware bays. Individual rows are separated by 1px hairline rules (`#1E2127`).
- Hover and focus states light the entire row to Surface Level 2 (`#1F2228`) with an immediate 3px vertical Crimson guide bar anchored to the far-left border.
